# Nanoleaf Hue Bridge Wiki

Nanoleaf Hue Bridge receives Sync+ colors from a Nanoleaf 4D and streams them to a Philips Hue Entertainment area. The Debian service handles the stream; the web portal and optional Home Assistant integration provide controls.

- [Installation and pairing](https://github.com/Cristef/nanoleaf-hue-bridge/wiki/Installation)
- [Troubleshooting FAQs](https://github.com/Cristef/nanoleaf-hue-bridge/wiki/FAQs)
- [README and portal screenshots](https://github.com/Cristef/nanoleaf-hue-bridge#readme)
- [Releases](https://github.com/Cristef/nanoleaf-hue-bridge/releases)

For everyday use, leave Debian running and use **Synchronization** in the portal, Desktop or Home Assistant. Use the portal for Hue mapping and profiles. Opening the mobile Sync+ editor can remove the virtual receiver from the 4D, even without saving; the FAQ explains how to recover.

The bridge is available under PolyForm Noncommercial. The separate [Home Assistant integration](https://github.com/Cristef/nanoleaf-hue-bridge-ha) uses MIT.
